UNIT 6 · WRITING SKILLS

A little gratitude goes a long way.

Learn to write a warm, polite thank-you letter to a host family.

Start the lesson ↓Go to planningGo to writing
STEP 01 · 3 MIN

A warm welcome

Imagine you have spent three weeks with your English penfriend’s family.

Talk together:

1. What could a host family do to make you feel welcome?

2. How could living with them help your English?

Useful words: host family · stay · hospitality · grateful
STEP 02 · 3 MIN

Read a thank-you letter

Read the letter. What is Mia thanking the family for? How did her English improve?

Dear Mr and Mrs Green,

I am writing to thank you for your kindness during my stay. I had a wonderful time with your family.

I really appreciated the comfortable room and the lovely meals. It was kind of you to show me around your town.

My English improved because we spoke together every day. Our conversations helped me feel more confident.

Thank you once again for your hospitality. I hope you can visit my family one day.

Best regards,
Mia

Check together

Mia thanks the family for the room, meals, sightseeing and hospitality. Speaking with them every day helped her English and confidence.

STEP 03 · 3 MIN

Build your letter

Look at Mia’s letter again. Find each part and say what it does.

1 · OPENING

Say why you are writing and thank the family.

2 · THANK THEM

Give examples: the room, meals or activities.

3 · EXPLAIN

Say how your English improved.

4 · CLOSE

Thank them again and invite them to visit.

Start: Dear Mr and Mrs Green,   ·   Finish: Best regards, + your first name

Semi-formal means friendly, polite and respectful. Avoid slang such as “Thanks a million!”

STEP 04 · 3 MIN

Say it politely

Choose the better phrase for a semi-formal letter. Then make a sentence about your stay.

1 · SAY WHY YOU ARE WRITING

A. I thought I’d drop you a line.

B. I am writing to thank you.

2 · THANK THE FAMILY

A. Thanks a million!

B. I really appreciated your kindness.

3 · THANK THEM AGAIN

A. Thank you once again for your hospitality.

B. You guys are awesome!

Try: It was kind of you to …

Check your answers

1–B · 2–B · 3–A. Example: It was kind of you to show me around your town.

STEP 05 · 2 MIN

Plan your own letter

Imagine you spent three weeks with your penfriend’s family. Choose a detail for each line.

1. Reason: Thank them for …

2. Examples: I enjoyed the … / It was kind of you to …

3. English: My English improved because …

4. Closing: Thank them again and invite them to …

Teacher: ask the student for one specific detail, such as a meal or a trip.

STEP 06 · 4 MIN

Write your first draft

Write your letter to the family. Aim for 70–100 words in four short paragraphs. Finish after class if needed.

Proofread & share
